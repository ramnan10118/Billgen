# RavenLog — Indian Document Generator Market Research

## Executive Summary

India's salaried workforce of 50M+ employees submits expense proofs twice a year
creating permanent cyclical demand for document generators. The most searched
document types cluster around three workflows: HRA rent receipts (Sec 10(13A)),
fuel/travel reimbursements (Sec 10(5) and 10(14)), and 80C/80D investment proofs.

Top three document types (rent receipts, fuel bills, salary slips) are saturated
by ClearTax, Tax2Win, Razorpay, NoBroker. A significant cluster of mid-demand,
low-competition document types remains underserved — RavenLog's entry opportunity.

## Strategic Tiers

### Tier 1 — Traffic Anchors (Build, compete on execution)
Rent receipts, salary slips, fuel bills.
Must be included — users expect all-in-one platform to cover these.
Differentiate through: superior design, GST field handling, revenue-stamp-ready PDFs.

### Tier 2 — Quality Gap Plays (High demand, mediocre competition)
LTA, medical, driver salary, hotel, cab bills.
Existing tools deliver poor output — generic formats lacking GST, GSTIN, Section compliance.
RavenLog's design-first approach dominates here.

### Tier 3 — Underserved Niches (Low competition, permanent demand)
Internet bills, home loan statements, tuition receipts, books/newspaper, toll, gym.
Each has thousands of monthly searches with almost no quality tools.
These are RavenLog's clearest first-mover opportunities.

## WFH Tailwind

Post-pandemic employer reimbursement policies now commonly include:
- Internet / broadband
- Electricity (partial)
- Home office equipment

Internet/broadband bill generator is the highest-opportunity niche given:
- WFH normalization — permanent shift not temporary
- Section 17(2) compliance gap in existing tools
- Almost non-existent quality competition

---

## Key Insight

The compliance gap is RavenLog's actual moat — not just design.

Existing tools generate generic PDFs with:
- No GSTIN fields
- No PAN fields
- No revenue stamp space
- No Section labeling
- No HSN/SAC codes

Building these in from day one separates RavenLog from every existing free tool.
